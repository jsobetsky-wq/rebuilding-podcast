# LinkedIn Content Strategy — Rebuilding a Broken Man

## Overview

John is already active on LinkedIn as an electronic security authority. The podcast introduces a personal brand dimension alongside the professional one. The goal is not to replace the security content — it's to add a human layer that deepens trust and expands reach.

## Content Pillars

### 1. Episode Promotion (1 post per episode)
Drop each week on the day the episode goes live. Not a generic "new episode!" post — pull a specific line or moment from the episode and let it do the work.

**Format:**
```
[Hook line — something real from the episode]

[2-3 sentences of context]

[Link to episode]

Episode [X]: "[Title]" — Rebuilding a Broken Man podcast

#RebuildingABrokenMan #Podcast
```

**Example:**
```
"I kept thinking if I just worked harder, closed more deals, stayed busier —
I wouldn't have to deal with what was actually wrong."

That's from the first episode of my new podcast. I own a security company.
I've built something I'm proud of. But I spent years pretending the rest
of my life was as solid as my business.

This is me, being honest about what that cost.

Episode 1: "Why I'm Here"
[link]

#RebuildingABrokenMan #Podcast
```

### 2. Micro-Lessons (1-2 per week)
Short-form posts that share a single insight from the rebuilding process. These don't directly promote the podcast but build the brand. Personal, specific, conversational.

**Topics that work:**
- A lesson from a hard week
- Something your kids taught you
- A habit that's making a difference
- An honest reflection on failure
- What running a business while rebuilding teaches you

### 3. Behind the Scenes (1 per week, optional)
Quick posts about the process — recording, what you're thinking about for the next episode, lessons from putting yourself out there.

**Example:**
```
Recorded Episode 4 yesterday. Had to stop and restart three times.

Not because I messed up the audio. Because I kept trying to
sound like I had this figured out.

The best take was the one where I didn't.
```

## Posting Schedule

| Day | Content Type |
|-----|-------------|
| Monday | Episode drop + promotion post |
| Wednesday | Micro-lesson post |
| Friday | Behind-the-scenes or second micro-lesson (optional) |

## Rules

1. **Never sacrifice the security brand.** The podcast is personal brand. It coexists with, not replaces, the professional authority.
2. **No motivational platitudes.** Specific > generic. Always.
3. **Don't over-promote.** One direct episode plug per week max. Let the personal content drive curiosity.
4. **Engage in comments.** When people respond — and they will — engage genuinely. This is where relationships deepen.
5. **Cross-pollinate carefully.** Occasionally bridge — "Running a security company while rebuilding yourself teaches you that protection starts from the inside." But don't force it.

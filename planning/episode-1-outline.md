# Episode 1: Why I'm Here — Full Outline

**Target Length:** 10–12 minutes
**Recording Platform:** Riverside.fm
**Format:** Solo, single take with light editing

---

## Cold Open (0:00–0:45)
No music. No intro. Just start talking.

> "I've been thinking about doing this for a while. And every time I sat down to plan it out, I talked myself out of it. Because who wants to hear a 40-something security company owner talk about how his life fell apart? But that's exactly why I'm doing this. Because I know I'm not the only one."

Pause. Let it breathe.

---

## Intro (0:45–1:15)
Short intro with music.

> "Welcome to Rebuilding a Broken Man. I'm John Sobetsky. I own an electronic security company called Main Line Security Solutions out of the Philadelphia area. And this podcast is about what happens when the guy who's supposed to have it all together... doesn't."

---

## Section 1: Who I Am on Paper (1:15–3:00)
Brief background — keep it conversational, not a resume:
- Own a national electronic security company
- Built the business from the ground up
- Cameras, access control, alarm systems — I protect other people's stuff for a living
- From the outside, things look solid

Key line to hit:
> "I'm the guy clients trust to keep their buildings safe. But I couldn't keep my own life from falling apart."

---

## Section 2: What Happened (3:00–5:30)
This is the real part. Be honest without oversharing on episode one. Leave room for future episodes.
- Things started cracking — personally, relationally, mentally
- The gap between the public version of me and the real version got too wide
- Running a business while your personal life is on fire
- The moment I realized I couldn't outwork this problem

Key line:
> "I kept thinking if I just worked harder, closed more deals, stayed busier — I wouldn't have to deal with what was actually wrong. That strategy works until it doesn't."

---

## Section 3: Why a Podcast (5:30–7:30)
- Not a therapist, not a guru, not selling a course
- Just a guy who's in the middle of rebuilding and willing to talk about it
- Men especially don't talk about this stuff — and it's killing us
- Found that when I started being honest, other guys said "me too"

Key line:
> "I'm not on the other side of this. I'm in it. And maybe that's the whole point — you don't need to be healed to be helpful."

---

## Section 4: What to Expect (7:30–9:00)
- ~10 minute episodes, weekly
- Solo format — just me and a microphone
- Topics: identity, failure, fatherhood, business, physical health, mental health, faith, relationships
- Raw and honest. Some episodes will be heavy. Some will be lighter.
- No guests for now — just me

Key line:
> "If you're a man going through something and you feel like you can't talk about it — this is for you. If you're rebuilding something and it feels impossibly slow — this is for you."

---

## Outro (9:00–10:00)
- Follow on Spotify, Apple Podcasts, wherever you listen
- Find me on LinkedIn — John Sobetsky
- Next episode: "The Mask We Wear"
- Sign off:

> "Thanks for being here. Seriously. This is the hardest thing I've done — and all I did was hit record. I'll see you next week."

Outro music.

---

## Production Notes
- Record in one take if possible. Authenticity > polish.
- Light editing only — remove long dead air, mouth noises. Keep the pauses.
- No sound effects or transitions between sections. Just music for intro/outro.
- Target loudness: -16 LUFS (podcast standard)

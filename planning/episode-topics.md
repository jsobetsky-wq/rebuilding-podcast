# Episode Topics — Rebuilding a Broken Man

## Season 1: The Foundation (Episodes 1–15)

### Episode 1: Why I'm Here
The origin story. What broke, why I'm doing this, and what to expect. Raw introduction to the podcast and to me.

### Episode 2: The Mask We Wear
The version of yourself you show the world vs. the real one underneath. The cost of keeping up appearances as a business owner and a man.

### Episode 3: Rock Bottom Is a Foundation
Reframing the lowest point — not as the end, but as the first solid ground you've stood on in years.

### Episode 4: The 3 AM Conversations
The thoughts that come when the world is asleep. Learning to sit with discomfort instead of running from it. What the quiet hours teach you.

### Episode 5: Ownership Without Excuses
Taking full responsibility for your life — not as punishment, but as power. The difference between blame and accountability.

### Episode 6: Building a Business While Broken
Running a company when your personal life is falling apart. How I kept Main Line Security Solutions going when I could barely keep myself going.

### Episode 7: The People Who Stay
Not everyone leaves when things get hard. A tribute to the people who stuck around, and what loyalty actually looks like when it's tested.

### Episode 8: Fatherhood in the Wreckage
Being a dad when you feel like you're failing at everything else. How kids see through your armor and why that's both terrifying and healing.

### Episode 9: The Physical Rebuild
How physical health became the first domino. Getting your body right when your mind is still a mess. Discipline as therapy.

### Episode 10: Letting Go of Who You Were
Grief for the person you used to be. Why clinging to your old identity keeps you stuck. The freedom in starting over.

### Episode 11: Asking for Help (And Why It's the Hardest Thing)
Men don't ask for help. Until they have to. How I learned to let people in, and what happened when I finally did.

### Episode 12: The Daily Grind of Getting Better
Recovery isn't a montage. It's doing boring, hard things every single day. The unglamorous reality of rebuilding.

### Episode 13: Trust — Rebuilding What You Destroyed
How trust breaks, how it heals, and why you can't rush it. The patience required to earn it back.

### Episode 14: Finding Purpose in the Pain
Not "everything happens for a reason" — but finding meaning you can create from what you've been through.

### Episode 15: What Rebuilding Actually Looks Like (Six Months In)
A check-in. What's changed, what hasn't, and what I've learned so far. Honest accounting of progress.

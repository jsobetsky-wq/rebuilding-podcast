# Intro & Outro Scripts

## Standard Intro (~30 seconds)

> You're listening to Rebuilding a Broken Man. I'm John Sobetsky — business owner, father, and a man putting the pieces back together one day at a time. This podcast is about what it actually takes to rebuild your life when everything you thought you had figured out falls apart. No filters, no quick fixes — just the truth.

---

## Standard Outro (~15 seconds)

> Thanks for listening. If this hit home, share it with someone who needs to hear it. Find me on LinkedIn or at rebuildingabrokenman.com. New episodes every week. Keep building.

---

## Episode 1 Intro (Custom — No Standard Intro)

Episode 1 uses a cold open instead. See `episode-1-outline.md` for details.

---

## Recording Notes

- **Intro music:** Starts under the last line of the intro, fades out as episode begins
- **Outro music:** Fades in under "Thanks for listening," plays out 5-10 seconds after final word
- **Pacing:** Read naturally, not rushed. The intro should feel like a conversation starting, not a commercial.
- **Music style:** Something atmospheric and moody — ambient, low-key. No corporate motivational music. Think post-rock or minimal piano.

# EP3 LinkedIn Posts, Rock Bottom Is a Foundation

## POST 1: Launch Day

No one is coming to save you. Not your parents, not your wife, not your friends, not God. He already did his part.

If you don't like your job, change it. Your body, change it. Your mindset, change it. Your marriage, change it. Your life, change it. All of those are a choice.

Most men are stuck because they're waiting. Waiting isn't a plan. It's a fantasy.

I went from almost 400 pounds to 280. I get up at 3:45 every morning. None of that happened by mistake. It happened because I stopped waiting and started building.

Rock bottom isn't the end. It's a foundation. Build from it.

## POST 2: Midweek Follow-Up

You're going to want to quit. Good. Do it anyway.

Everyone has bills. Everyone is tired. Everyone has excuses. The difference is what you do about it.

Rock bottom is a foundation. Build from positivity, build from God, build from hard work, build from honesty.

You're not done. You're just starting.

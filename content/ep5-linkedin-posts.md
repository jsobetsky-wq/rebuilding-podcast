## POST 1

If you want to change something, you have to change something. That is a you problem. Only you can do that.

I put my career above my family and my wife. And that got me to where I was broken. I stopped doing that. Making conscious choices to put God and my family first has changed everything.

You can break any cycle. Your parents were alcoholics? Don't drink. You don't like your body? Change it. You don't like your job? Change it. Simple in concept. Hard in practice.

Consistency, faithfulness, times time, equals lasting impact. I've been reading the Bible every day. Praying every day. Putting my wife and my family first every day. My life is improving. No coincidence.

I have found my calling. Helping men who are in the thick of it. Who are struggling. Who don't see a way out. Those are my people. If that is you, reach out.

Start today. Start right now. Rock bottom is not the end. It is the starting point.

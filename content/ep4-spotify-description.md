# Episode 4: Move Different, Spotify Description

## Title
Move Different

## Description

Coming in hot today. Stream of consciousness. A lot on my mind.

Father forgive them, for they not know what they do. That hit me on my morning walk. What is your Jerusalem? What is the direction you're working towards that you feel like you're falling short of?

Mine is simple. Leave this earth better than how I found it. Break the generational cycles in my family for my children and the generations to come. And stand up against the work and societal cultures that tell men to speak up, then hold it against them when they do.

My faith in the Lord has gotten stronger than ever. God has been putting breadcrumbs in my path for years. Jason, Joe, the men's group, Troy, Jared. That men's group in April changed my life. From that point forward I just decided to move totally different.

The driving force behind everything I do is being a husband and a father. It's behind me finding God. Behind me going to the gym. Behind me working the way that I do. I haven't been the perfect husband or the perfect father. But there's never been a perfect man to walk this planet except for one.

You've got to actually move different. You've got to actually be different. A lot of people want to say a lot but they don't want to do a lot. Iron sharpens iron. Be better. Just be better. Every day, incrementally. 1% better. The only competition you have is the person you were yesterday.

Happy Father's Day to all the fathers out there. Rock bottom isn't the end. It's the starting point.

# Episode 3: Rock Bottom Is a Foundation, Spotify Description

## Title
Rock Bottom Is a Foundation

## Description

No one is coming to save you. Not your parents, not your wife, not your friends, not God. He already did his part. Now it's on you.

If you don't like your job, change it. Your body, change it. Your mindset, change it. Your life, change it. All of those are a choice. Most men are stuck because they're waiting. Waiting isn't a plan. It's a fantasy.

I went from almost 400 pounds to 280. I get up at 3:45 every morning. I'm training for a 5K. None of that happened by accident. It happened because I stopped waiting for someone to save me and started building.

Rock bottom isn't the end. It's bedrock. It's the foundation. Build from it. Build from positivity, build from God, build from hard work, build from honesty.

You're going to want to quit. Good. Do it anyway. You're not done. You're just starting.

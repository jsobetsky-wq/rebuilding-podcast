## Status: Planning
## Target Date: TBD (after EP2 launch June 1)

## Concept

Rock bottom isn't the end of the story. It's the foundation you build on. Most men hit a point where everything falls apart — the relationship, the job, the identity, the self-respect. Society tells you that's failure. This episode reframes it: rock bottom is where the rebuilding starts because there's nothing left to pretend about.

## Key Themes

- Rock bottom as a starting point, not an ending
- Stripping away the fake version of yourself
- The freedom in having nothing left to lose
- Building from zero vs. maintaining a lie
- What "foundation" actually looks like — daily choices, accountability, showing up

## Talking Points

1. **What rock bottom looked like** — be specific, be real
2. **The moment of clarity** — when you stopped fighting the fall and started building
3. **What you lost vs. what you gained** — the trade was worth it
4. **Foundation = daily discipline** — not a single moment, it's bricks every day
5. **Message to the guy at rock bottom right now** — you're not done, you're just starting

## Tone

Raw. No sugarcoating. Same energy as EP1 and EP2. This is the guy who's been there talking to the guy who's there now.

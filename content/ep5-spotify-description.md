# Episode 5: Start Today, Spotify Description

## Title
Start Today

## Description

This episode is about making a change. If you want to change something, you have to change something. If you want to do something, you have to do something. That is a you problem. Only you can do that.

In the past, I put my career above my family and my wife. And that got me to the point where I started this podcast. It got me to where I was broken. But I stopped doing that. Making conscious choices to put God and my family first has led me on a better path. No coincidence.

You can break any cycle. Any situation you're in, you can change it. You don't like your job? Change it. You don't like your body? Change it. Simple in concept, hard in practice.

Consistency, faithfulness, times time, equals lasting impact. I've been reading the Bible every day. I've been praying every day. My life is improving.

I have found my calling. I believe it is to help men who are in the thick of it. Who are struggling, who are depressed, who are suicidal. Those are my people. If that is you, reach out.

Start today. Start right now. Rock bottom is not the end. It is the starting point.

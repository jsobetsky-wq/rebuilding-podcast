# Episode 2: Men's Mental Health — Spotify Description

## Title
Men's Mental Health

## Description

June is Men's Mental Health Awareness Month, and I'm not sugarcoating it.

Most of us are hard men raised by hard men, and that hasn't done us any favors when it comes to emotional stability or mental health. I bottled it up for years — suicidal thoughts, extreme anger, the whole thing. I'm fortunate to still be here.

This episode is me being blunt about what happens when men suffer in silence, especially in blue collar trades and first responder communities where the suicide rate is out of control. I talk about finding outlets, doing the uncomfortable self-reflection, and why the strongest men I know are the ones who finally got vulnerable about what they were going through.

Broken is not the end. It is the starting point.

If you're struggling, reach out to someone. If that someone is me, then it's me. Just talk to somebody.

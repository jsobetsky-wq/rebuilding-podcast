## POST 1: Launch Day (June 1st, 6:00 AM)
**Type:** Episode Promotion
**Publish:** Same time as Spotify drop

---

June is Men's Mental Health Awareness Month.

I recorded Episode 2 of Rebuilding a Broken Man because this topic almost killed me. Literally.

Most of us are hard men who were raised by hard men. That didn't teach us how to process anything — it taught us to bottle it up. And sometimes bottling it up came in the form of suicidal thoughts. Sometimes it came in the form of extreme anger that just builds and builds until it explodes.

I'm fortunate to still be here through the suicide attempts. Not everyone is.

The suicide rate in the trades and first responder communities is out of control and it needs to stop. Society bears blame. The industry bears blame. But so do we.

This month isn't about weakness. It's about the courage to stop hiding, face what's broken, and rebuild.

Broken is not the end. It is the starting point.

## POST 2: Midweek Follow-Up (June 3rd or 4th)
**Type:** Clip Promotion / Micro-Lesson

---

The strongest men I know are the ones who did the self-reflection.

They evaluated themselves. They got uncomfortable. They got vulnerable about what they were going through.

And they are now better men, better fathers, better husbands, better employees — better period — because of it.

You don't have to figure it all out today. But you do have to start. Spend 5 or 10 minutes by yourself. Check in. Ask yourself the hard questions. Where am I at? Why do I do this? What is the root cause?

You're not going to like it. It's going to suck. Too bad — get over it.

It's better than not being here.
